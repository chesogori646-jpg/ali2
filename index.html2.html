What you can do in the app

  • Browse tasks like data entry, app testing, image labelling and opinion surveys
  • Work at your own pace, with no fixed hours
  • Track completed tasks and your balance
  • Request M-Pesa payouts when eligible

How it works

  1. Install the app from Google Play
  2. Check the reward shown before you start each task
  3. Complete tasks (they are reviewed before they count)
  4. Read the withdrawal requirements, then request your payout
